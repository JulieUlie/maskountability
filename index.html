Company claims, checked against the evidence

Each entry is one specific public claim by a company, with the evidence we reviewed and the verdict. Verdicts describe the evidence, not the character of the company. Records are reviewed before they are published, and changes are logged.

How to read a record
  1. Each post is archived, so the claim can't quietly change.
  2. The evidence is listed, including what it can't prove.
  3. A verdict says whether the evidence supports the claim. It doesn't judge the company's character.
  4. Records are reviewed before they are published, and each change is logged.

Records are versioned. Corrections and withdrawals are shown in each record's history.

Send a claim

Tell us about a specific public claim a company has made, and the evidence you have for or against it. Every submission is read by a person before anything is published. Submissions are not published automatically.

Before you send

  • Companies and brands only. Don't send claims about private individuals, and remove personal details such as home addresses, phone numbers, or family information from your evidence.
  • Quote the claim exactly and link to the original post if you can.
  • Describe evidence, not motives. Say what the evidence shows and what it can't prove.
  • Only send what you're comfortable being shared with the company if it's reviewed.

Other places to report

Some issues belong with an official body. You can report them directly:

  • Misleading advertising, including undisclosed influencer posts: Jury voor Ethische Praktijken inzake Reclame (JEP)
  • Other consumer and business practices: FOD Economie
  • Company registration details: KBO public search
  • Personal data concerns: Gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit
Submissions are stored privately and reviewed by a person. Nothing is published automatically.
